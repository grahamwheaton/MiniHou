# Viewport selection tools

Region gestures support Box, Lasso, and Circle. Circle paints while left drag is held and interpolates strokes to avoid gaps; use its wheel to adjust the radius from 4 to 300 CSS pixels. Shift adds to box/lasso selection; Ctrl or the middle button subtracts. Circle adds by default. Enter finishes Circle. Escape or right click cancels the current session and restores the selection from before it began. Text fields retain their key input.

Region selection follows the existing box semantics: projected points, edges whose two endpoints are contained, and triangle centres for faces. In solid display it selects visible elements; X-ray or a wireframe display mode permits through selection. The wireframe overlay on solid display remains depth tested. Selection respects projection/section filtering supplied by the viewport and is not full Blender selection parity.

The most-direct route uses world-space element positions: points, edge midpoints, or face centres. Its graph-search cost is normalized segment length weighted by distance from the straight line joining the chosen endpoints, plus an angular bend penalty. This prefers a straight detailed chain over a fewer-hop bevel detour. It is a geometric preference on connected topology, not an edge-loop recognizer; faces and irregular meshes can still have ambiguous alternatives. Disconnected endpoints produce no route. The separate Shortest Path action preserves the minimum-hop topology route.
