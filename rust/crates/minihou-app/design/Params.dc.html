Rules

A parameter is modified when it differs from the node type's default. The dot and the reset button depend on that alone. Edits apply on drag with the cook debounced, so the viewport follows the slider and the cook indicator shows in the status bar. Invalid values are kept in the field, outlined red, and explained under the group. They do not reach the engine. The disabled reason is always the tooltip text, never a separate label in the row. Groups open or closed state belongs to the node type, not to each node.
